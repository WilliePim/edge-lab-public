Item 2.01 Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets. Shortly thereafter, on January 4, 2016, the Merger was completed pursuant to Section 251(h) of the DGCL, with no stockholder vote required to consummate the Merger. At the Effective Time, the Company became an indirect, wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. As a result, a change of control of the Company occurred.
