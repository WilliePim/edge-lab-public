# Bibliography: corporate events, forced selling and insider purchases

The academic references consulted while designing the scanner and the studies in this repository, grouped by
topic. It is a reading list, not a ranking: it says nothing about which topics this project studies, in what order,
or how they turned out. Bibliographic details are given as recorded in the working notes: where the notes carry a
title, journal and pages, they are reported; where they carry only authors and year, only those are reported. Entries
marked *(not verified)* were noted from memory or secondary sources and must be checked against the original.
